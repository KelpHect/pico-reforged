---
title: Overflow auto
description: Let wide content such as tables scroll inside its box.
---

`.overflow-auto` sets `overflow: auto` on an element. Wrap content that can be wider than the page, most often a table, and it scrolls inside the wrapper instead of stretching the layout.

<Example title="Overflow auto" height={200}>
```html
<div class="overflow-auto" style="width: 20rem; outline: 1px dashed gray">
  <div style="width: 40rem">A wide block inside .overflow-auto, wider than its box.</div>
</div>
```
</Example>

## Tables

<Example title="Scrollable table" height={260}>
```html
<div class="overflow-auto">
  <table>
    <thead>
      <tr>
        <th scope="col">Planet</th>
        <th scope="col">Diameter (km)</th>
        <th scope="col">Distance from Sun (AU)</th>
        <th scope="col">Moons</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr>
        <th scope="row">Mercury</th>
        <td>4,880</td>
        <td>0.39</td>
        <td>0</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <th scope="row">Earth</th>
        <td>12,742</td>
        <td>1.00</td>
        <td>1</td>
      </tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</div>
```
</Example>

## Accessibility

A scrollable region can be hard to reach with a keyboard. If the wrapper holds nothing focusable, give it `tabindex="0"`, `role="region"` and an `aria-label` so keyboard users can scroll it and screen readers announce it.

The class has no variables. See also [Table](/docs/content/table).
